# Controlled video collection checklist

- [ ] Site owner has authorized the exact camera position and recording period.
- [ ] Every visible participant has received the study purpose, camera/data use, retention and deletion date, access list, withdrawal process, and contact details in writing.
- [ ] Written consent is stored separately from video and annotation identifiers.
- [ ] Recording area excludes nonconsenting bystanders; collection stops if one enters.
- [ ] Scenarios are benign staged movements; no weapon use, confrontation, panic induction, or physical intervention.
- [ ] Camera, lighting, viewpoint, frame rate, and clip/session IDs are documented.
- [ ] Raw video storage is access-restricted; transfer and deletion owner are identified.
- [ ] Evidence-clip face blur is visually reviewed; unblurred raw clips are not shared.
- [ ] Retention deadline and deletion log are recorded.
- [ ] Applicable institutional and data-protection review is complete before collection.
